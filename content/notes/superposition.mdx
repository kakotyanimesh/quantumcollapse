So, let's start with two properties of electrons

<ol type="1" className=" ">
  <li>Hardness</li>
  <li>Color</li>
</ol>
which means an electron can either be hard or soft and either white or black. We
have machines that can gives us the idea of electrons hard/soft and white/black
properties. Those machines have a magnetic field $\mathbf{B} = B_0 \mathbf{e}_z$
for color and $\mathbf{B} = B_0 \mathbf{e}_x$ for hardness, which is known as
magnetic field induced splitting.
