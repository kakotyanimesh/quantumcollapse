title: "Why I'm building this site"
description: "A short note on why I'm putting my physics notes online instead of keeping them in a drawer."
date: "2026-01-05"
tags: ["meta"]
---

I've been keeping physics notes in notebooks for a while now, and most of
them never get looked at again once the semester ends. This site is an
attempt to fix that.

The plan is simple: write up what I'm learning as I learn it, keep the notes
short enough that I'd actually want to re-read them, and collect the
resources that actually helped instead of the ones that just looked good on
a syllabus.

Nothing fancy — just notes, a few longer posts when I have something worth
saying, and a resources page I'll keep updating.
